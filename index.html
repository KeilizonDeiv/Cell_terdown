Cell vs. cell

Bigger cells hold more energy each. The fairer test is energy per kilogram and per liter, where the lightweight pouch leads per kilogram, the tightly wound 4680 leads per liter, and LFP trades density for long life.

Discharge curve

Voltage drops as a cell empties. NMC slopes down steadily; LFP holds a flat ~3.3 V almost to the end, which is why its charge level is harder to read from voltage alone.

Typical curve shapes at a moderate load. Real curves shift with temperature, age and current.
